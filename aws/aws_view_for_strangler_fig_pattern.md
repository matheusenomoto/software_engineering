## Overview

Nowadays, due to business strategies (not only business logic of a system), modern frameworks, etc... An monolithic application migrating to cloud became very commom into modern approach to achieve system resilience, availability, security and more.

A microservice architecture incrementally have become a good way to do it, with lower transformation risk and business disruption.

I wrote a agnostic article about Strangler Fig Pattern [link](https://github.com/matheusenomoto/software_engineering/blob/main/Systems%20Design/Strategy/strangler_fig_pattern.md).

## Why

Application designed as a monolithic application has a tightly coupled code. As a result, any change requires thorough retesting, it cannot be tested in isolation, "hardtime with cycle time", higher complexity...???
