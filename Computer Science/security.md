**Network Security**

* **HTTPS**
  * Scrambles all data. HTTPS protects the data sent between your browser and a website, so no one can spy on, tamper with, or impersonate the communication.

<img width="868" height="73" alt="http_https_workflow" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/697ba918-c45d-47f9-b101-6fa7a2d8a01f" />

* **Virtual Private Network VPN**
  * A VPN hides your online activity, encrypts your internet connection, and can make it look like you're browsing from another location.
  * A VPN can operate at different layers of the OSI or TCP/IP networking models, depending on the type of VPN protocol being used.

It adds extra steps to protect data — and those steps take time and resources. **_trade-offs_**
